ImagetoMap

Turn a photo's GPS location into a downloadable map snapshot — runs entirely through your browser, nothing is stored on the server.

1. Choose upload mode

2. Select an image

2. Select images

Folder selection is supported in Chrome and Edge. If your browser doesn't support it, use "Select files..." and multi-select instead.

Detected images

Images without GPS location data (common with WhatsApp photos, screenshots, or re-saved images) need coordinates entered manually as decimal degrees, e.g. 32.7266 / 74.8570. Leave both blank to skip that image.
